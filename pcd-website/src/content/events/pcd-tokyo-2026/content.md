In 2026, the open-source creative coding tool Processing will celebrate its 25th anniversary. To commemorate this milestone, Processing Community Day (PCD) events will be held around the world. Here in Tokyo, we will host PCD Tokyo 2026 on Saturday, October 24.

PCD is a community event where people from diverse fields—including artists, designers, engineers, and educators—who create using Processing and p5.js come together to network and showcase their work. We welcome everyone interested in creative coding, from beginners to experienced users.

PCD Tokyo 2026 will take place across two venues:
-GARAGE AKIHABARA will host the main interactive program, featuring a keynote address, lightning talks, workshops, and networking opportunities.
-NEORT++ will host the exhibition, showcasing physical and interactive installations alongside digital screenings.
