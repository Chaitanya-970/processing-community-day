Thanks to everyone who showed up for Seattle's Processing Community Day in Seattle.  If you want more PCD activities please come to the [PNW Art & Tech Symposium October 24th](https://artandtechsymposium.com/), and free exhibit running October 24th-31st.  Several artists in the exhibit are using Processing and/or other open source tools, including:

- Joel Lamberg, Light Beyond Reach
- Joseph Gray, Subtle Chroma (PNW ATS 2026)

- Kevin Landesman, Gumdrop
- Raphael Fortuna, Tactile Reactive Relief Of The Great Wave Off Kanagawa After Hokusai

- Yan Shao, Algae Chorus
- Yuhanxiao (Maggie) Ma & Anna Ren, The Guinea Pig Eligibility Test

​The [Seattle Processing Community Day ](https://day.processing.org/event/pcd-seattle-2026-5ea5839/?utm_source=luma)meetup is open to anyone interested in the [Processing](https://processing.org/?utm_source=luma) programming language and creative code in general. The Processing language is 25 years strong in 2026 and this event joins many across the globe.

​We'll be chatting about Processing, and other open source coding tools. Plans will be made here for activities like [zine creation](https://day.processing.org/organize/zines/zine-library/?utm_source=luma) and other collaborations.

​We'll also talk about the upcoming [PNW Art & Tech Symposium](https://artandtechsymposium.com/?utm_source=luma) and inviting folks to submit work for a Processing focused part of the symposium exhibition.

​You can join our discord group via the link at [seattlecreativecode.com](https://seattlecreativecode.com/?utm_source=luma) to be a part of the conversation.
